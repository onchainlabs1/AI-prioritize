What This Tool Solves

Purpose

The workbench helps enterprise teams avoid AI hype decisions by applying a clear method:

  • collect initiatives with structured business context;
  • filter ideas that should not use AI (Stage 0);
  • apply route and region policy before prioritization;
  • enforce governance gates before scoring;
  • prioritize with weighted criteria and evidence quality;
  • record board decisions with audit trail.

Decision Logic Summary

Method
Stage 0

Route the initiative as GenAI/RAG, agentic, classical ML, deterministic, or not suitable.

Policy

Stage 0 route and regulatory region apply minimum gate states, threshold deltas, and tier caps.

Scoring

Weighted criteria plus evidence multipliers produce a decision score and a confidence index.

Current Policy Layers

Model
Stage 0 route policy

`agentic` is stricter than `genai_rag`, and `not suitable` becomes immediate `NO-GO`.

Region policy

`EU`, `US`, `EU + US`, and `Global` each apply different minimum gate expectations and thresholds.

Gate behavior

`Fail` blocks immediately. `Conditional` can reduce the score and cap the maximum tier.

Evidence behavior

Weak evidence reduces criterion contribution and also lowers the reported confidence index.

How To Read Results

Interpretation
  • `Tier A`: prioritize now, assuming policy and gates allow it.
  • `Tier B`: plan next, usually with more discovery or controls.
  • `Tier C`: incubate or rework before portfolio commitment.
  • `NO-GO`: blocked by Stage 0, failed gates, or unmet policy baseline.
  • When blocked, the tool shows a diagnostic score for context, not as an approval signal.

Key Outputs

Executive View
  • Priority lane (`Tier A`, `Tier B`, `Tier C`, `NO-GO`)
  • Decision score or blocked diagnostic score
  • Explainable score breakdown
  • Gate, policy, and rationale summary
  • Board decision history
  • Audit trail per initiative
