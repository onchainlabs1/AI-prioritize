What This Tool Solves

Purpose

The workbench helps enterprise teams avoid AI hype decisions by applying a clear method:

  • collect initiatives with structured business context;
  • filter ideas that should not use AI (Stage 0);
  • enforce governance gates before scoring;
  • prioritize with configurable weighted criteria;
  • record board decisions with audit trail.

Workflow Diagram

Decision Flow
1

2

Triage Queue

Reviewers filter by status, unit, lane, and owner to route work.

3

Stage 0 + Gates

Check AI appropriateness and mandatory Pass/Conditional/Fail gates.

4

Weighted Scoring

Score impact, feasibility, economics, risk, and readiness.

5

Board Decision

Approve, hold, reject, or approve with conditions and rationale.

Decision Logic Summary

Method
Stage 0

Does this initiative really need AI, or should it be automation/analytics first?

Gates

Fail blocks progress. Conditional applies penalty and policy tier limits.

Scoring

Weighted criteria + evidence confidence produce final lane recommendation.

Key Outputs

Executive View
  • Priority lane (`Tier A`, `Tier B`, `Tier C`, `NO-GO`)
  • Explainable score breakdown
  • Gate status and rationale
  • Board decision history
  • Audit trail per initiative
